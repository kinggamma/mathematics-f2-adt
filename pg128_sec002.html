Example 6.6

Express each of the following equations according to the given instruction:

  • (a) log2⁡8=3 in exponential form.
  • (b) 5−3=1125 in logarithmic form.
  • (c) 0.1=10−1 in logarithmic form.

Solution

  • (a) log2⁡8=3 in exponential form is written as 23=8.
  • (b) 5−3=1125 in logarithmic form is −3=log5⁡(1125).
  • (c) 0.1=10−1 in logarithmic form is −1=log10⁡(0.1).
