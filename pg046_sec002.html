Example 3.1

Given that ΔSLK~ΔNFR, identify all the corresponding angles and the corresponding sides.

Solution

Using the order of vertices of the two similar triangles, SL̂K corresponds to NF̂R, KŜL corresponds to RN̂F, and LK̂S corresponds to FR̂N.

Also, LS of ΔSLK corresponds to FN of ΔNFR, SK of ΔSLK corresponds to NR of ΔNFR, and KL of ΔSLK corresponds to RF of ΔNFR.
