Example 5.34

Simplify 273÷643.

Solution

Write the numbers under the radical symbol as in products of prime factors as follows:

273÷643=3×3×33÷2×2×2×2×2×23
=(3×3×32×2×23)÷2×2×23
=32×2×23
=34
