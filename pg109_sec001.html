Example 5.27

Express 10245 in its simplest form.

Solution

Write 1024 as a product of prime factors.

1024=2×2×2×2×2×2×2×2×2×2
=25×25

Apply the fifth root on both sides to obtain,

10245=25×255
=(25)15×(25)15
=2×2
= 4

Therefore, 10245=4.

Example 5.28

Express each of the following numbers in its simplest form.

(a) 20
(b) 543

Solution

(a)20=2×2×5
=2×2×5
=2×5
=25

Therefore, 20=25.

(b)543=3×3×3×23
=3×3×33×23
=3×23
=323

Therefore, 543=323.

Example 5.29

Express each of the following numbers under a single radical sign:

(a) 23
(b) 243

Solution

(a)23=2×2×3
=12

Therefore, 23=12.

(b)243=2×2×2×43
=323

Therefore, 243=323.
