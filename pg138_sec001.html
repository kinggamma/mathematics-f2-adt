Example 6.21

Given,

log10⁡2=0.30103, log10⁡3=0.47712,
log10⁡ 5 = 0.69897,\ and  log10⁡ 7 = 0.845098

Evaluate each of the following:

(a)
log3⁡7
(b)
log5⁡12
(c)
log7⁡15
(d)
log6⁡20

Solution

(a) log3⁡7=log10⁡7log10⁡3
=0.8450980.47712=1.77124
(b) log5⁡12=log10⁡12log10⁡5=log10⁡(22×3)log10⁡5
=2log10⁡2+log10⁡3log10⁡5
=2×0.30103+0.477120.69897=1.55396
(c) log7⁡15=log10⁡15log10⁡7=log10⁡(3×5)log10⁡7
=log10⁡3+log10⁡5log10⁡7
=0.47712+0.698970.845098=1.39166
(d) log6⁡20=log10⁡20log10⁡6=log10⁡(22×5)log10⁡2+log10⁡3
=2log10⁡2+log10⁡5log10⁡2+log10⁡3
=2×0.30103+0.698970.30103+0.47712=1.67195
