Congruence

Example 2.9

In the following figure, prove that △RVS ≅ △TVU.

Solution

Given that RS//UT and RS=UT. Required to prove that △RVS ≅ △TVU.

Proof: In △RVS and △TVU, it implies that

SŔV = UŤV (alternate angles, RS//UT).

RS=UT (given)

RŜV = TÛV (alternate angles, RS//UT)

Therefore, △RVS ≅ △TVU (by ASA postulate).
