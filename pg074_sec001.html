Note that, from the general quadratic expression ax2+bx+c, where a, b and c are real numbers.

(a) If a=0, the expression becomes linear. For example, 6x+4, in this case, a=0, b=6 and c=4.

(b) If b=0, the expression is a quadratic but without the middle term. For example, 4x2+8, in this case, a=4, b=0 and c=8.

(c) If c=0, the expression is a quadratic but without the constant term. For example, 10x2−2x, in this case, a=10, b=−2 and c=0.

(d) The basic identities of quadratic expressions are as follows:

  • (a+b)2=a2+2ab+b2
  • a2−b2=(a+b)(a−b)
  • (x+a)(x+b)=x2+(a+b)x+ab
