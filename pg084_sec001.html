Example 4.35

What must be added to x2−52x to make it a perfect square?

Solution

The coefficient of x is −52. Half of −52 is 12(−52)=−54. The square of −54 is 2516. Therefore, 2516 must be added to x2−52x to make it a perfect square.

Example 4.36

Solve the quadratic equation x2+5x−14=0 by completing the square.

Solution

The equation x2+5x−14=0 has to be rearranged so that the left-hand side is converted to a perfect square. From x2+5x−14=0. It follows that,

x2+5x=14

Add the square of half the coefficient of x on both sides to get,

x2+5x+254=14+254

Factorize the LHS to obtain,

(x+52)2=56+254
(x+52)2=814

Taking square root on both sides gives,

x+52=±814
x+52=±92

Thus, x=−52±92. Either x=−52+92 or x=−52−92. Thus, x=2 or x=−7. Therefore, x=2 or x=−7.

Example 4.37

Example 4.38

Solve the quadratic equation 3x2−7x−6=0 by completing the square.

Solution

Given 3x2−7x−6=0. Divide each term by 3 to get,

x2−73x−2=0
x2−73x=2
x2−73x+4936=2+4936
(x−76)2=72+4936=12136
x−76=±12136=±116
x=76±116

x=7+116 or x=7−116

Thus, x = 3 or x=−23.

Therefore, x = 3 or x=−23.

Example 4.39

Solve the quadratic equation 2x2+7x+6=0.

Solution

Given 2x2+7x+6=0.

Divide each term by 2.

x2+72x+3=0

Subtract 3 from both sides,

x2+72x=−3

Add (74)2 on both sides.

x2+72x+(74)2=−3+(74)2
(x+74)2=−3+4916=−48+4916
(x+74)2=116

Take the square root on both sides:

x+74=±116=±14

Subtract 74 on both sides to get,

x=−74±14=−7±14

x=−7+14=−32 or x=−7−14=−2

Therefore, x=−32 or x = -2.
