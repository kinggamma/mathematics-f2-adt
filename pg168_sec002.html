Activity 8.1: Measuring heights using shadows

1. Explore the basic trigonometric ratios (sine, cosine, and tangent) and their real life applications.

2. On a sunny day, place a long stick vertically on the ground, then measure the height and length of its shadow.

3. Measure the shadow of a tall object such as a tree or building and use similar triangles to estimate its height.

4. Share your findings with the class, explaining your process and justifying your results.
