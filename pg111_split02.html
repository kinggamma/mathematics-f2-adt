Example 5.32

Simplify each of the following expression products:

(a) 3×5

(b) 2×32

(c) 20×28

(d) 12(3+5)

(e) (23−2)×(3+32)

Solution

(a) 3×5=15

(b) Given that 2×32, it follows,

32=2×2×2×2×2=42

Thus, 2×32=2×42

=4×2×2

=4×2

=8

(c) Given 20×28, it follows that,

20=2×2×5=25

28=2×2×7=27

Thus, 20×28=25×27

=2×2×5×7

=45×7

=435

(d) 12(3+5)=4×3(3+5)

=23(3+5)

=23×3+23×5

=2×3+215

=6+215

(e) Given (23−2)×(3+32)

Expand as follows;

(23−2)×(3+32)=
(23×3)+(23×32)+(−2×3)
+(−2×32)
=29+66−6−34
=2×3+56−3×2
=6+56−6
=6−6+56
=56

Therefore,

(23−2)×(3+32)=56.
