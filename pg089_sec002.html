Chapter summary

1. A binary operation is a rule for combining two quantities to produce a unique quantity from the same set.

2. An expression whose highest power of the variable is 2 is called a quadratic expression.

3. Factorisation is the process of writing an expression as a product of two or more factors.

4. A quadratic equation is formed when a quadratic expression is equal to a specific value, typically zero.

5. The solutions of quadratic equations are known as roots.
