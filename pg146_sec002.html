Activity 7.1: Sorting items having the same characteristics

1. Gather a variety of items such as exercise books, pens, pencils, erasers, rulers, notebooks, highlighters, and paperclips.

2. Sort the items based on their specific characteristics.

3. How many different groups of items have you created?

4. Assign each group a letter: A, B, C, etc.

5. Reflect on the similarities and differences between the groups. Consider the characteristics that influenced your sorting decision.
