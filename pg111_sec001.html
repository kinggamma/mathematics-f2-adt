Example 5.31

Simplify each of the following expressions:

(a)
67−27
(b)
6285−28965
(c)
324−25924
(d)
2+168075+2435

Solution

(a)
67−27=47
(b)
6285−28965
=6285−(22×2×2×2×2×285)
=6285−(2×2×2×2×25×285)
=6285−(2×2×285)
=6285−4285
=2285
(c)
324−25924
324=2×2×2×2×24=224
25924=6×6×6×6×24=624
324−25924=224−624
=−424
(d)
2+168075+2435
168075=7×7×7×7×75=7
2435=3×3×3×3×35=3
2+168075+2435=2+7+3
=12
