Suppose the expression (24)3 is to be written as a single exponent, then it can be written in expanded form as follows:

(24)3=24×24×24
=24+4+4
=24×3
=212

Therefore, (24)3=24×3=212.

Similarly, consider a number (a2)4, where a is any real number. The number can be written as a single exponent as follows:

(a2)4=a2×a2×a2×a2
=a2+2+2+2
=a2×4
=a8

Therefore, (a2)4=a2×4=a8.

Generally, (xm)n=xmn, where m and n are positive integers and x is any real number. This law is known as the power law.

Example 5.7

Simplify each of the following by using power law of exponents:

(a)\ (3^6)^3
(b) ((37)6)2
(c)\ (0.7^7)^3

Solution

(a) (36)3=36×3=318
(b) ((37)6)2=(37)6×2=(37)12
(c) (0.77)3=(0.7)7×3=0.721

Furthermore, the expression (7×5)3, can be written in expanded form as follows:

(7×5)3=(7×5)×(7×5)×(7×5)
=7×5×7×5×7×5
=7×7×7×5×5×5
=73×53

Therefore, (7×5)3=73×53.

Similarly, if a and b are real numbers, then (a×b)4 can be expanded as follows:

(a×b)4=(a×b)×(a×b)×(a×b)×(a×b)
=(a×a×a×a)×(b×b×b×b)
=a4×b4

Therefore, (a×b)4=a4×b4.

Generally, (a×b)n=an×bn, where a and b are real numbers and n is a positive integer is known as the power law of a product.
