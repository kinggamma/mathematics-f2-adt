Right angle-Hypotenuse-Side (RHS) postulate

Two right-angled triangles are congruent if their hypotenuses have equal length and a pair of the corresponding sides have equal length. Figure 2.7 illustrates the postulate.

Figure 2.7 shows that,

AB = PQ (given)

AC = PR (given)

ÂBC = P̂QR = 90° (given).

If two triangles satisfy the conditions in the RHS postulate, then the triangles must fit exactly. Thus, Figure 2.7 shows that △ABC ≅ △PQR (by RHS).

Since the two triangles in Figure 2.7 are congruent, then, all the corresponding sides and angles are equal. That is,

BC = QR

BÂC = Q̂PR

BĈA = Q̂RP.
