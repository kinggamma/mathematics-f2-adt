Applications of Logarithms

Logarithms are used in solving problems involving exponential relationships. Logarithms are widely applied in real-life situations such as measuring pH in chemistry, calculating compound interest in finance, and determining earthquake magnitudes.

Example 6.24

Solve the equation 7x+1=90. Give you answer into 4 decimal places.

Solution:

Given 7x+1=90. Taking logarithm on both sides gives,

log⁡(7x+1)=log⁡90
log(7x+1) = log 90
(x+1)log⁡7=log⁡90
(x+1)log 7 = log 90
x+1=log⁡90log⁡7,x=log⁡90log⁡7−1
x + 1 = log 90log 7, x = log 90log 7 - 1

Using a calculator, x=1.3124.
