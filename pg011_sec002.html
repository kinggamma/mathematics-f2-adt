Exchange rates

In any country, people expect to do transactions in the currency of their own country. When money from country A is to be used in country, it is necessary to exchange the currency of country A to the currency of country B. Various currencies in the world are linked together by exchange rates. This enable smooth transfer of money and payments to take place between countries.
