Consider △PMN and △LPN.
M̂NP = L̂NP (common).

M̂LP = P̂LN (common).
L̂MP = N̂PL = 90° (given).

△LMP ∼ △LPN (2).

△ LPN ∼ △ PMN ∼ △ LMP
