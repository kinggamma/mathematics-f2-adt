Example 6.7

Solve for x in each of the following equations:

  • (a) x=log10⁡100
  • (b) −5=logx⁡(0.00001)
  • (c) log8⁡x=2

Solution

  • (a) Given x=log10⁡100.
    Writing in exponential form gives 100=10x.
    Thus, 102=10x.
    Equating exponents gives x=2.
  • (b) Given −5=logx⁡0.00001.
    Writing in exponential form, gives x−5=0.00001.
    But 0.00001=10−5.
    Thus, x−5=10−5.
    Therefore, x=10.
  • (c) Given log8⁡x=2.
    Writing in exponential form gives 82=x.
    Therefore, x=64.
