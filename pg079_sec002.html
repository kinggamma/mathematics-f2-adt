Quadratic equations

In the previous section, you learned about quadratic expressions. A quadratic equation is formed when a quadratic expression is equal to a specific value. Quadratic equations take the general form
ax^2 + bx + c = 0.
The solutions of such an equation are also known as roots or zeros. There are also several methods for solving quadratic equations, including factorization, completing the square, and the general quadratic formula.
