3.

Relationship between trigonometric ratios for angle α.

(a)sin⁡α=cos⁡(90∘−α)
(b)cos⁡α=sin⁡(90∘−α)
(c)tan⁡α=sin⁡αcos⁡α
(d)sin2⁡α+cos2⁡α=1
(e)sin⁡(−α)=−sin⁡α
(f)cos⁡(−α)=cos⁡α
(g)tan⁡(−α)=−tan⁡α
4.

Angle of elevation and depression:

(a)Angle of elevation is the angle obtained when the line of sight is above a horizontal line.
(b)Angle of depression is the angle is obtained when the line of sight is below a horizontal line.
