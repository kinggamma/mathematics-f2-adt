Rates and variations

Student's Book Form Two

Introduction

Understanding rates and variations is essential for grasping how quantities change in relation to each other. Some variables are interdependent, meaning a change in one affects the others. Many real-life situations involve such relationships. In this chapter, you will describe the concepts of rates and variations, explain the types of variations, and solve problems on rates and variations. The competencies developed will enable you to solve real-life challenges such as finding shopping deals, assessing fuel efficiency, or predicting data trends in fields like economics, science, engineering, and many other applications.
