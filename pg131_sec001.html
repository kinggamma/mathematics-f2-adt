Example 6.8

Evaluate each of the following.

(a) log3⁡(81×9)

(b) log5⁡(125×625)

Solution

(a) log3⁡(81×9)=log3⁡81+log3⁡9

=log3⁡34+log3⁡32

=4log3⁡3+2log3⁡3

=(4×1)+(2×1)

=4+2

=6

Therefore, log3⁡(81×9)=6.

(b) log5⁡(125×625)=log5⁡125+log5⁡625

=log5⁡53+log5⁡54

=3log5⁡5+4log5⁡5

=(3×1)+(4×1)

=3+4

=7

Therefore, log5⁡(125×625)=7.

Example 6.9

Find the value of each of the following:

(a) log2⁡(4×8)

(b) log10⁡(0.01×100,000)

Solution

(a) log2⁡(4×8)=log2⁡4+log2⁡8

=log2⁡22+log2⁡23

=2log2⁡2+3log2⁡2

=(2×1)+(3×1)

=2+3

=5

Therefore, log2⁡(4×8)=5.

(b) log10⁡(0.01×100000)

=log10⁡0.01+log10⁡100000

=log⁡10−2+log⁡105

=−2log⁡10+5log⁡10

=(−2×1)+(5×1)

=−2+5

=3

Therefore, log10⁡(0.01×100,000)=3.

Power rule

Power rule states that

loga⁡mn=nloga⁡m.

Let

p=loga⁡m.

(i)

It is derived as follows: Express equation (i) in exponential form to get,

ap=m

(ii)

Raise both sides of equation (ii) to the power n to get,

apn=mn

(iii)

Apply logarithm to base a on both sides of equation (iii) to obtain,

loga⁡(apn)=loga⁡mn

(iv)

Simplify equation (iv) to get

pn=loga⁡mn.

But

p=loga⁡m.

It follows that,

nloga⁡m=loga⁡mn

Therefore,

loga⁡mn=nloga⁡m.

From this rule, it follows that

loga⁡1x=−loga⁡x

Example 6.13

Find the value of each of the following:

(a)log4⁡(64)5

(b)log⁡(100)25

(c)log⁡(0.1)6

Solution

(a)log4⁡(64)5=5log4⁡(64)

=5log4⁡43

=5×3log4⁡4

= 15

Therefore,

log4⁡(64)5=15.

(b)log⁡(100)25=25log⁡100

=25log⁡102

=25×2log⁡10

=25×2×1

= 50

Therefore,

log⁡(100)25=50.

(c) log⁡(0.1)6=6log⁡(0.1)

=6log⁡10−1

=6×(−1)log⁡10

=6×(−1)×1

= -6

Therefore,  log⁡(0.1)6 = -6.

Quotient rule

Let p=loga⁡x and q=loga⁡y.

(1)

Expressing equation (1) in exponential form gives,

x = ap  and  y = aq.

(2)

From equation (2), divide x by y to obtain

xy = ap ÷ aq = ap−q\ (by laws of exponents)

Thus,

xy=ap−q.

(3)

Express equation (3) in logarithmic form to obtain,

loga⁡(xy)=loga⁡ap−q

=(p−q)loga⁡a

Thus,

loga⁡(xy)=p−q.

(4)

Substitute the expressions for p and q from equation (1) into equation (4) to obtain,

loga⁡(xy)=loga⁡x−loga⁡y

Therefore,

loga⁡(xy)=loga⁡x−loga⁡y.

Example 6.14

Find the value of each of the following expressions:

(a) log3⁡(279).

(b) log3⁡(9÷243)

(c) log⁡(10÷0.001)

Solution

(a) log3⁡(279)=log3⁡27−log3⁡9

=log3⁡33−log3⁡32

=3log3⁡3−2log3⁡3

= 3(1) - 2(1)

= 1

Therefore,  \log_3\left(\frac{27}{9}\right) = 1.

(b) log3⁡(9÷243)=log3⁡9−log3⁡243

=log3⁡32−log3⁡35

=2log3⁡3−5log3⁡3

= 2(1) - 5(1)

= -3

Therefore,  log3⁡(9 ÷ 243) = -3.

(c) log⁡(10÷0.001)=log10⁡(100.001)

=log10⁡10−log10⁡0.001

=log10⁡101−log10⁡10−3

=1log10⁡10−(−3)log10⁡10

= 1 + 3 = 4

Therefore,  log⁡(10 ÷ 0.001) = 4.
