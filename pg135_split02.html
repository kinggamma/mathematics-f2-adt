Example 6.20

Evaluate each of the following:

(a) log2⁡32

(b) log5⁡125

(c) log3⁡81

(d) log4⁡16

=log10⁡25log10⁡2

=5log10⁡2log10⁡2=5

(b)log5⁡125=log10⁡125log10⁡5

=log10⁡53log10⁡5

=3log10⁡5log10⁡5=3

(c)log3⁡81=log10⁡81log10⁡3

=log10⁡34log10⁡3

=4log10⁡3log10⁡3=4

(d)log4⁡16=log10⁡16log10⁡4

=log10⁡42log10⁡4
