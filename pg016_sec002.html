Activity 1.4: Identifying inverse variations in daily life

  1. 1. Identify three real-life activities where increasing one variable decreases the other variable.

  2. 2. Perform one of the activities to experience how changes of the variables are related in the activity.

  3. 3. Find out through reading books and browsing the internet how to express these inverse relationships mathematically.

  4. 4. Use the mathematical expression to explain how changes in one variable affect the other, and present your findings.
