Example 8.14

Find the sine, cosine, and tangent of each of the following angles:

(a) 144° (b) −231° (c) −70° (d) 310°

Solution

(a)
sin⁡144∘=sin⁡(180∘−144∘)=sin⁡36∘=0.5878
cos⁡144∘=−cos⁡(180∘−144∘)=−cos⁡36∘=−0.8090
tan⁡144∘=−tan⁡(180∘−144∘)=−tan⁡36∘=−0.7265
(b)
sin⁡(−231∘)=sin⁡(360∘−231∘)=sin⁡129∘=sin⁡(180∘−129∘)=sin⁡51∘=0.7771
cos⁡(−231∘)=−cos⁡51∘=−0.6293
tan⁡(−231∘)=−tan⁡51∘=−1.2349
(c)
sin⁡(−70∘)=sin⁡(360∘−70∘)=sin⁡290∘=−sin⁡(360∘−290∘)=−sin⁡70∘=−0.9397
cos⁡(−70∘)=cos⁡70∘=0.3420
tan⁡(−70∘)=−tan⁡70∘=−2.7475
(d)
sin⁡(310∘)=−sin⁡(360∘−310∘)=−sin⁡50∘=−0.7660
cos⁡(310∘)=cos⁡50∘=0.6428
tan⁡(310∘)=−tan⁡50∘=−1.1918
