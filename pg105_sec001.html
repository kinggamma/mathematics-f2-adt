Example 5.17

Find the value of n in each of the following equations:

(a)
2n+1=64
(b)
4^n=16

Solution

(a)
2n+1=64

Express 64 in power form to obtain

2n+1=26

Comparing exponents gives,

n+1=6
n=5

Therefore, n=5.

(b)
4^n=16

Express in power form and compare exponents to obtain

4^n=4^2
n=2

Therefore, n=2.

Example 5.18

Find the value of b in each of the following equations:

(a)
b3=27
(b)
(b+1)^3=64

Solution

(a)
b3=27

Express 27 in power form to obtain,

b3=33
b=3

Therefore, b=3.

(b)
(b+1)^3=64

Write 64 in power form to get

(b+1)^3=4^3
b+1=4
b=4-1
b=3

Therefore, b=3.

Example 5.19

Solve for x and y if

2x×3y=144.

Solution

From

2x×3y=144.

Writing 144 in exponential form gives,

144=24×32

It follows that,

2x × 3y = 24 × 32,\; 2x = 24  and  3y = 32

Comparing exponents of the same base gives x=4 and y=2.

Therefore, x=4 and y=2
