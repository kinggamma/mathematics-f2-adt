Addition and subtraction of radicals

Two or more radicals can be added or subtracted if they are alike. Radicals which are alike are those with the same index and radicand. This means that radicals of the same index can be added or subtracted, just as it is done in algebraic expressions. The radicals 2 and 23 cannot be added or subtracted because have unlike indices. The radicals √2 and ∛2 cannot be added or subtracted because have unlike indices. Before adding or subtracting radicals, first simplify the terms if possible.
