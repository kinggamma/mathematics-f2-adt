Example 8.3

In a right-angled triangle ABC,

AB=3 cm,\ BC=4 cm,\ and\ AC=5 cm.

Find the value of each of the following:

  • (a) sin A
  • (b) cos A
  • (c) tan A

Solution

The given information is summarized in the following figure.

From the figure, ABC is a right-angled triangle such that, ∠ABC=90∘.

It follows that:

(a)\ sin⁡ A=Length of opposite sideLength of hypotenuse side
=BCAC=4 cm5 cm
Therefore, sin⁡ A=45.
(b)\ cos⁡ A=Length of adjacent sideLength of hypotenuse side
=ABAC=3 cm5 cm
Therefore, cos⁡ A=35.
(c)\ tan⁡ A=Length of opposite sideLength of adjacent side
=BCAB=4 cm3 cm
Therefore, tan⁡ A=43.
