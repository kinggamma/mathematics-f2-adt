Example 5.36

Rationalize the denominator in each of the following expressions:

(a) 35
(b) 15−3
(c) 55+3
(d) 2+32−5
(e) 67−2

Solution

(a) 5 is a denominator and a single radical expression.

Multiply both numerator and denominator by 5 .

35=35×55=355

Therefore, 35=355 .

(b) 15−3=15−3×5+35+3=5+3(5−3)(5+3)=5+35−3=5+32

Therefore, 15−3=5+32 .

(c) 55+3=55+3×5−35−3=5(5−3)5−3=5−152

Therefore, 55+3=5−152 .
