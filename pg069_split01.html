Transposition of formulae

A formula is an equation which shows how variables are related. For example, the formula which is used to find the circumference, C of a circle is given by C=πd, where C is expressed in terms of π and d. In this case, C is the subject of the formula. It is also possible to express d as the subject of the formula, that is, d=Cπ.

The procedure of expressing a formula in different ways is called transposition of formula or changing subject of the formula. The following are some examples of formulae:

(a) A=lb

(b) V=πr2h

(c) I=PRT100

(d) A=12(a+b)h

(e) T=2πlg

(f) y=mx+c

(g) s=ut+12at2
