Example 2.10

Use the following figure to prove that △ADB ≅ △ADC and DB = DC.

Solution

Given △ABC such that AD is perpendicular to BC.

Required to prove that

(a) △ADB ≅ △ADC

(b) DB = BC

Proof: From △ADB and △ADC, it implies that

AB = AC (given)

ÂDB = ÂDC = 90° (given)

AD is a common side.

Therefore, △ADB ≅ △ADC (by RHS), hence DB = DC (definition of congruence of triangles).
